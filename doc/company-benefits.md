# Axon Corp — Employee Benefits Guide

This document outlines the full benefits package available to all Axon Corp employees. Benefits are effective from the first day of employment unless otherwise noted.

---

## 1. Health Insurance

Axon Corp covers 100% of the employee's health insurance premium and 80% of dependent premiums. Coverage includes medical, dental, and vision. Employees may choose between two plans: the Standard PPO plan or the High-Deductible Health Plan (HDHP) paired with a company-funded Health Savings Account (HSA) of up to $1,500 per year.

---

## 2. Annual Leave

All full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per year, accrued monthly. After 5 years of service, this increases to 25 days. Unused leave of up to 10 days may be carried over to the following year. Leave must be approved by the direct manager at least 5 business days in advance.

---

## 3. Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. A fully remote arrangement may be requested after 6 months of employment, subject to manager and HR approval. The company provides a one-time home office setup allowance of $800 and a monthly internet stipend of $50.

---

## 4. Learning & Development Budget

Each employee receives an annual learning budget of $2,000 to spend on courses, certifications, conferences, or books relevant to their role. Requests are submitted through the HR portal. Unused budget does not roll over. Employees who leave within 6 months of a company-sponsored certification may be asked to reimburse the cost.

---

## 5. Parental Leave

Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Leave may be taken any time within the first 12 months after the birth or adoption of a child. Employees must notify HR at least 8 weeks before the planned start date.

---

## 6. Retirement Plan (401k)

Axon Corp offers a 401(k) retirement plan with a company match of up to 5% of the employee's salary. The match vests fully after 2 years of employment. Employees are automatically enrolled at a 4% contribution rate on their start date and may adjust this at any time through the benefits portal.

---

## 7. Mental Health Support

All employees have access to an Employee Assistance Programme (EAP) providing up to 8 free counselling sessions per year with a licensed therapist. The service is confidential and available 24/7 by phone, video, or in person. In addition, the company covers mental health visits under the standard health insurance plan with no additional co-pay.

---

## 8. Flexible Working Hours

Core working hours are 10:00 to 16:00. Outside of core hours, employees may flex their start and end times to suit their schedule, provided they complete their contracted hours each week. Compressed work weeks (e.g. 4×10-hour days) may be arranged with manager approval.

---

## 9. Wellness Allowance

Employees receive a monthly wellness allowance of $100, reimbursable for gym memberships, fitness classes, sports equipment, meditation apps, or other approved wellness activities. Claims are submitted through the HR portal with a receipt within 30 days of the expense.

---

## 10. Stock Options

All full-time employees are granted stock options equivalent to 10% of their annual base salary at the time of joining. Options vest over 4 years with a 1-year cliff. The strike price is set at the fair market value on the grant date. Details are provided in the individual option agreement signed at onboarding.

---

## 11. Commuter Benefits

Employees who commute to the office may claim up to $150 per month pre-tax for public transport passes or parking. Claims are processed through payroll and reduce taxable income. The benefit does not apply to remote-only employees.

---

## 12. Sabbatical Leave

Employees who have completed 5 years of continuous service are eligible to apply for a 4-week paid sabbatical. The sabbatical may be used for personal development, travel, or rest. Applications must be submitted 3 months in advance and are subject to business need and manager approval. Sabbatical leave resets after each use and becomes available again after a further 5 years.

---

## 13. Equipment & Technology

Every employee receives a laptop of their choice (MacBook or ThinkPad, up to $2,500) refreshed every 3 years. Additional peripherals such as a monitor, keyboard, and headset are provided on request. Employees working remotely may request equipment to be shipped to their home address.

---

## 14. Company-wide Holidays

In addition to annual leave, all employees receive 12 paid public holidays per year. The specific dates follow the local public holiday calendar of the employee's country of employment. Two additional floating holidays are granted each year for use on culturally or personally significant dates of the employee's choice.

---
